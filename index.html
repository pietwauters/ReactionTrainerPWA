Audio settings

iPhone: when on, your music keeps playing, but the silent switch mutes this app.

Plays a very quiet noise during an exercise so Bluetooth speakers and headsets don't go into power-saving mode and miss the start of a signal.

Music from e.g. Spotify plays softer while an exercise or the metronome runs, so the signals stand out.
